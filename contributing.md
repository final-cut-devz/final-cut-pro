## Pro Tips 💡

1. **Organize Media First** - Use Keywords and Smart Collections before starting your timeline edit.
2. **Use External NVMe SSDs** - Store large video libraries on fast APFS-formatted external SSDs.
3. **Master Keyboard Shortcuts** - Learn `B` (Blade), `E` (Append), `W` (Insert), and `Q` (Connect) for rapid cutting.
4. **Use Proxy Media on Laptops** - Edit smoothly on battery power with lightweight proxies, then switch to Original Media for export.
5. **Clean Render Files Regularly** - Delete unused generated render files after finishing a project to save disk space.
6. **Check Video Scopes** - Use Waveform and Vectorscope monitors rather than relying solely on screen brightness.
7. **Assign Audio Roles** - Tag clips as Dialogue, Music, or Effects for easy volume balancing and stem exports.
8. **Use Compound Clips** - Group complex multi-layer sequences (`Option + G`) to keep your main timeline clean.
9. **Backup Libraries Automatically** - Keep Final Cut Pro's automatic library backups enabled on a separate drive.
10. **Download From Official Sources** - Always install Final Cut Pro from the Mac App Store to ensure stability and updates.
